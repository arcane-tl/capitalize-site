Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 September 2026

Product: Capitalize

This policy describes how Capitalize handles information. Capitalize is a local-first app: your asset and maintenance data stays on your device unless you export or share it yourself.

1. Who we are

Capitalize is published by Digileap.

For questions, see Support or email capitalize@digileap.fi.

2. What Capitalize does

Capitalize helps you track personal assets (for example vehicles, homes, or electronics) and related maintenance. Version 1 is designed to work on your device without a Capitalize account and without uploading your portfolio to a Capitalize cloud.

3. Data stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Capitalize may store on your device:

  • Asset details you enter (names, notes, values, dates, and similar fields)
  • Maintenance tasks and schedules
  • Photos or attachments you choose to add
  • App preferences (for example reminders or app lock)
  • Data needed for optional app lock (PIN / biometric unlock), handled through the operating system’s secure APIs

Version 1 does not upload your portfolio to a Capitalize server for sync, backup, analytics, or advertising.

4. On-device encryption

On supported builds, Capitalize stores application data in an on-device encrypted database. The encryption key is kept in the operating system’s secure storage (Keychain / Keystore), not in ordinary app files.

Encryption protects the database file at rest on the device. It is not a guarantee against every form of compromise (for example malware running with your privileges on an unlocked device).

5. App lock (PIN and biometrics)

You may enable an optional app lock. When enabled, Capitalize can require a PIN and, where available, device biometrics (such as Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint) to open the app.

Biometric checks are performed by the device and operating system. Capitalize does not receive your fingerprint or face template.

6. Notifications and reminders

Capitalize can schedule local reminders for upcoming maintenance. Those notifications are created and delivered by the operating system on your device.

In version 1:

  • Reminders are local-only
  • Capitalize does not operate a Capitalize push server for these reminders
  • Capitalize does not send your device’s remote push token to a Capitalize notification service for these reminders

The app binary may include a push-notifications capability required by the platform for signing or SDK use. That capability does not mean Capitalize runs a remote push service or collects push tokens for Capitalize servers in version 1.

7. Photos and library access

If you grant photo library access, Capitalize uses it so you can attach pictures of assets or receipts. Those files stay on your device unless you export or share them yourself. Version 1 does not host your media on Capitalize servers.

8. What we do not do (version 1)

  • No Capitalize cloud account
  • No cloud sync of your portfolio
  • No Capitalize remote push service for maintenance reminders
  • No sale of your portfolio data to third parties

9. This website

These pages provide product and legal information. They do not require an account to read. Ordinary web server connection logs may be processed by the host that serves the pages, under that host’s own policies.

10. Children

Capitalize is not directed at children. Do not use the app to store sensitive information about minors beyond what your device’s own controls already allow.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when the product changes. Material updates will revise the effective date on this page.

12. Contact

Privacy questions: capitalize@digileap.fi
