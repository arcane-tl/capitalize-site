User guide

Get started with Capitalize: add assets, log maintenance, and keep reminders on your device.

Getting started

  1. Install Capitalize from the App Store.
  2. Add an asset (for example a vehicle or appliance) with the details you care about.
  3. Log maintenance tasks and due dates so you can track work over time.
  4. Optionally enable app lock (PIN and biometrics) to protect the app when you leave your phone.
  5. Optionally enable local reminders so your device notifies you before maintenance is due.

How your data is kept

Your portfolio is meant to stay on your device in an encrypted database. Version 1 reminders are local notifications on the phone — not messages from a Capitalize cloud.

There is no Capitalize account and no cloud sync in version 1.
