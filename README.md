# Capitalize

Capitalize helps you track personal assets and maintenance on your phone.

Your portfolio stays on your device in an encrypted database. You can lock the app with a PIN or biometrics. Maintenance reminders in version 1 are local notifications on the device — not remote pushes from a Capitalize server.

There is no Capitalize cloud account and no cloud sync in version 1.
